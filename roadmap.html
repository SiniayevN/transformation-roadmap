Sephora Digital Transformation

A dependency-aware roadmap for creating a personalized, data-driven omnichannel beauty experience — from reliable customer data to advanced AR and AI-powered commerce.

Transformation Goal
Create a personalized, data-driven omnichannel beauty experience.

Transformation Journey

FOUNDATION → EXPERIENCES → INTELLIGENCE
CRAWL
Months 0–9
Foundations
1

Customer Data Foundation

Build reliable customer and loyalty data.

Dependency: Foundation for later customer-data initiatives.

2

Unified Customer Profiles

Connect customer information across digital and in-store touchpoints.

Dependency: Depends on Customer Data Foundation.

3

Digital Innovation Capability

Build the capability to test and scale new digital experiences.

Dependency: Supports the Walk and Run initiatives.

WALK
Months 6–18
Experiences
4

Omnichannel Customer Experience

Connect stores, mobile, web, loyalty, and services.

Dependency: Depends on Unified Customer Profiles + Digital Innovation Capability.

5

Color iQ Personalization

Use technology-assisted matching to improve product discovery.

Dependency: Builds on customer data and digital infrastructure.

RUN
Months 15–30+
Intelligence
6

Virtual Artist / AR Try-On

Scale virtual product experimentation and connect discovery to purchase.

Dependency: Builds on mature digital commerce + personalization.

7

AI-Powered Personalized Commerce

Use AI for more intelligent product discovery and recommendations.

Dependency: Depends on integrated data + mature digital experiences.

Main Dependency Sequence

Customer Data Foundation→Unified Customer Profiles→Omnichannel Customer Experience→Color iQ Personalization→Virtual Artist / AR Try-On→AI-Powered Personalized Commerce

Why Crawl Comes First

We begin with customer data, connected profiles, and digital innovation capability because these create the foundation for later customer-facing technologies.

Why Walk Comes Next

The Walk phase uses those capabilities to strengthen omnichannel experience and personalization.

Why Run Comes Last

The Run phase scales more advanced AR and AI experiences after integrated data and mature digital experiences are in place.

Roadmap note: This roadmap represents our proposed sequencing of Sephora's digital initiatives based on dependencies rather than the historical order in which they were launched.
