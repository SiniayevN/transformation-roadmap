Target Audience

Our target audience is the Sephora executive responsible for digital transformation and customer experience. This leader needs to decide how Sephora should prioritize investments in customer data, omnichannel experiences, personalization, AR, and AI while making sure each initiative has the right foundation to succeed.

Benefits

This project helps leadership understand which digital initiatives should come first and which depend on earlier capabilities. The Roadmap shows the recommended transformation sequence, the RACI identifies ownership for important governance decisions, and the QBR Dashboard provides a quick way to monitor progress, adoption, and business outcomes.

Novelty Statement

Our project goes beyond simply listing Sephora's digital initiatives by showing how they depend on each other. We placed customer data and unified customer profiles in the Crawl phase because advanced personalization cannot work effectively without a strong data foundation. We then placed omnichannel experiences and Color iQ in Walk before moving into more advanced capabilities such as Virtual Artist and AI-powered personalized commerce.

Our dashboard also focuses on adoption and customer engagement, not just whether a technology has been launched. This allows Sephora leadership to see whether customers and employees are actually using the new capabilities and whether they are producing results. Together, the roadmap, RACI, and dashboard provide a practical way to prioritize initiatives, assign decision ownership, and track whether Sephora's digital transformation is progressing successfully.
